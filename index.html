About

I am a computational chemist working on molecular dynamics simulations, force-field development, and electronic-structure calculations, applied to self-assembling soft materials, antibacterial nanomaterials, smart membranes for desalination, and lubricant design.

Previously I was a Staff Scientist at the Qatar Environment and Energy Research Institute (QEERI, 2014–2020), a Foreign Researcher at P. J. Šafárik University in Košice, Slovakia (2022–2023), and a Visiting Scholar at Northwestern University (2021–2022). I did postdoctoral work with George C. Schatz (Northwestern, 2007–2013) and Jeffery G. Saven (University of Pennsylvania, 2002–2007), and earned my Ph.D. in Physical Chemistry from Seoul National University (2002, advisor Doo Soo Chung).

Research Interests

  • Design of lubricants based on metallocene poly-α-olefin
  • Protein design and folding simulation with AI algorithms
  • Amyloid fiber formation and secondary-structure transitions of peptides
  • Self-assembly of peptide-based soft materials
  • Antibacterial activity of multifunctional nanomaterials (MXene)
  • Phase transition and separation in lipid membranes
  • Seawater desalination by smart membranes

European Grants Open to Korea-Based Applicants

A working survey (Oct 2025 – Oct 2026) of Europe-wide funding a Korea-based researcher or company can apply for. Dates are indicative — always verify on the official portals before planning an application.

Key context

  • Since 1 Jan 2025, Korea is associated to Horizon Europe Pillar II: Korean legal entities can receive EU funding directly and can coordinate Pillar II consortia.
  • ERC: open to any nationality, but the grant must be hosted at an EU/Associated Country institution (a host can be arranged).
  • EIC (Pathfinder/Accelerator): applicants must be established in the EU/Associated Countries — a Korean company cannot apply directly.
  • Eurostars / Eureka: the Korean partner is funded by KIAT (Korean government) after international selection; a KOITA-accredited corporate R&D centre is required — already held.
